# Inspiration Document Template (`docs/inspiration.md`)

Use this structure when creating `docs/inspiration.md`. Fill in gaps from the user's prompt with thoughtful, realistic product details, and explicitly list inferred assumptions.

```markdown
# <Project / Feature Title> — Inspiration & Vision

## 1. Executive Summary
- **One-Line Pitch:** <Crisp summary of what this is and who it helps>
- **Problem Statement:** <What pain point or gap exists today?>
- **Proposed Solution:** <High-level vision of the product/feature>

## 2. Target Users & Personas
- **Primary Persona:** <Role, context, goals, frustrations>
- **Secondary Persona(s):** <Optional additional users/admins/integrators>

## 3. Core User Journeys & "Aha" Moments
1. **<Journey 1 Name>:** <Step-by-step narrative of how the user accomplishes their goal>
2. **<Journey 2 Name>:** <Step-by-step narrative>

## 4. Feature Landscape
### Must-Have Capabilities (Core)
- <Capability 1 and why it matters>
- <Capability 2 and why it matters>

### Nice-to-Have / Future Possibilities
- <Future idea 1>

## 5. Edge Cases & Experience Details
- <How should errors, empty states, permissions, or scale be handled from a user's perspective?>

## 6. Constraints, Assumptions & Non-Goals
- **Assumptions Made (Inferred Gaps):**
  - <Assumption 1>
- **Explicit Non-Goals (Out of Scope):**
  - <What we are deliberately NOT building>

## 7. Success Criteria
- <Observable outcomes that prove the idea succeeded>
```
